I am a junior PI at ICMM-CSIC, where I lead the spin qubit research line within the <a href='https://wp.icmm.csic.es/tqe/'>Theory of Quantum Materials for Quantum Technologies group</a>. My team works on semiconductor-based quantum computation and hybrid quantum devices. Before starting my own line at ICMM, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the CEA-Grenoble in the <a href='https://www.mem-lab.fr/L_SIM'>L-Sim group</a> from 2020 to 2023, and at the University of Wisconsin-Madison in the <a href='https://pages.physics.wisc.edu/~friesen/group.html'>Silicon Qubit Theory Group</a> from 2018 to 2020.

Our research focuses on quantum computation in condensed matter systems. In particular, we develop theory for the quantum phenomena that naturally arise in realistic devices, connecting directly with experimental results. We perform analytical and numerical calculations of such phenomena to optimize quantum operations, in close collaboration with leading experimental groups across Europe. You can meet the people behind this research in <a href='/team/'>The team</a> section.
